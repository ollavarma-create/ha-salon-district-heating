# Tariffs (rc.4)

Optional settings via integration Configure. Enter VAT-inclusive EUR/MWh and EUR/month. Enable next tariff and choose a future date, or turn scheduling off to cancel it. Current and next sensors resolve from a date-versioned schedule using HA local time, including after downtime. Initial prices apply from configuration day; no historical import. Hourly energy cost uses the reported consumption timestamp's local calendar date, not retrieval time. Interval start/end semantics remain unconfirmed; boundary costs are estimates. No accumulated or monthly cost ledger yet. Existing recorded energy statistics are unchanged. Example: 75.94 EUR/MWh and 34.05 EUR/month; from 2027-01-01, 79.69 EUR/MWh and estimated 34.73 EUR/month. No prices are preconfigured. Test options, cancellation, midnight transition, restart, delayed readings and translations before production.
