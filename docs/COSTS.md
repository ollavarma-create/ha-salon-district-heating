# Cost estimates (v0.1.0)

Only confirmed hourly records at or after the first enabled observation hour are recorded. UTC timestamp keys prevent duplicates; overlapping 48-hour queries can recover missed or corrected records within that window. Longer gaps are not imported. The original recorded price is retained on corrections. Storage is per config entry and removed on integration removal. Monthly energy and hour count show coverage. Basic fee is a full-month estimate (calendar-day weighted when all dates are known, otherwise current fee). Total and effective price include that full fee even for partial months; not billing data. No historical import or external tariff lookup. Timestamp interval semantics remain provisional. Dashboard entity IDs are examples and may require adjustment. Fedora field testing confirmed accumulating costs over 32 recorded hours. Production validation and real calendar tariff transition remain pending. The effective price may be unusually high early in a month or when monitoring starts mid-month, because the entire monthly fee is included. It decreases as recorded consumption accumulates; compare complete months with adequate coverage.
